# Programme Overview

## Purpose

This framework helps **software developers create and execute their own Professional Development Plans (PDPs)**, with a focus on **Platform Engineering and its enabling disciplines**.

It provides a recommended learning pathway of approximately **571–753 study hours**, study materials, practical exercises and tools that developers can adapt to their experience, goals and available time.

## What is Platform Engineering?

**Platform Engineering is the practice of building and maintaining internal tools and services that make it easier for developers to deliver software.** It brings infrastructure, automation, security and operations together into reliable, reusable, self-service capabilities.

Instead of every development team solving the same deployment and infrastructure problems, a platform provides common solutions—such as automated environments, deployment workflows, service templates and observability.

### Four connected disciplines

| Discipline | Contribution |
| --- | --- |
| **DevOps Engineering** | Version control, containers, CI/CD, infrastructure as code, GitOps and observability |
| **Cloud Engineering** | Linux, networking, cloud services, infrastructure and architecture |
| **AI Engineering** | Applied LLMs, agentic workflows and LLM operations |
| **Platform Engineering** | Kubernetes, developer self-service, internal platforms and reliability |

**Why this order?** DevOps establishes the delivery foundation. Cloud adds the infrastructure. AI introduces intelligent capabilities. Platform Engineering brings them together into services that make development easier and safer.

### Platform Engineering specialisations

Platform Engineering spans several related areas:

| Specialisation | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Infrastructure | Cloud platforms, provisioning and orchestration |
| Developer Experience | Golden paths, templates, portals and self-service |
| Security | Identity, secrets, policy and supply-chain controls |
| Reliability | Observability, resilience, SLOs and incident response |
| Data | Shared data infrastructure and services |
| Platform Leadership | Platform strategy, adoption and product thinking |

The framework provides a foundation across these areas; it does not claim to produce an expert in every specialisation.

## Final Project

The recommended pathway includes a **Final Project**: progressively building an **Internal Developer Platform** that brings together the skills developed across DevOps, Cloud, AI and Platform Engineering.

Individual modules contain **Practical Assessments** to demonstrate specific capabilities; the Final Project brings those capabilities together. Developers can adapt the project or choose equivalent practical work that fits their own PDP.

See the [Final Project guide](1.4%20Final%20Project.md) for the suggested scope, stages and completion criteria.

## Certifications

**This framework does not award any certifications**, and obtaining certifications is **not required** to create, follow or complete a PDP.

For developers who want formal recognition of their skills, the framework also provides **optional guidance on relevant industry certifications**. These can be included in a personal PDP according to individual goals, interests and resources. Any certification must be earned separately through its issuing organisation.

## What success looks like

A developer using this framework should have a **personalised, actionable PDP**: clear development goals, identified skill gaps, selected learning materials and a realistic plan for making progress.

They should also have access to the **study materials, practical exercises and projects** needed to execute that plan, rather than having to build a learning programme from scratch.

As they complete their chosen materials, they should be able to **demonstrate the skills they have developed** through working code, infrastructure, deployments and other practical evidence. Developers who complete the full recommended pathway should have the foundation to build and operate an Internal Developer Platform.
