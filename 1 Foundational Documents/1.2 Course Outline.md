## How the learning builds

- **DevOps — 120–150 hours:** Build, package, deploy and observe a sample application.
- **Cloud — 128–160 hours:** Run it on secure, resilient cloud infrastructure.
- **AI — 96–120 hours:** Add a carefully scoped AI capability and learn how to evaluate and operate it.
- **Platform — 168–210 hours:** Turn the accumulated capabilities into a reusable Internal Developer Platform.

The [Final Project](1.4%20Final%20Project.md) grows throughout the pathway; the final learning activities involve **technical implementation**, not administration-only tasks.

**Study material maturity:** The [DevOps syllabus](../3%20Study%20Materials/3.1%20DevOps/DevOps_Syllabus.md) contains detailed learning objectives and labs. The Cloud, AI and Platform module files are **working outlines** and still need equivalent detail. Their current titles and estimated hours establish the intended sequence, not a completed syllabus.
