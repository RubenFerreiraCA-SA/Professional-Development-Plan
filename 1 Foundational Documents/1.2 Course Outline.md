## How the learning builds

- **DevOps — 179–263 hours:** Build, package, deploy and observe a sample application.
- **Cloud — 128–160 hours:** Run it on secure, resilient cloud infrastructure.
- **AI — 96–120 hours:** Add a carefully scoped AI capability and learn how to evaluate and operate it.
- **Platform — 168–210 hours:** Turn the accumulated capabilities into a reusable Internal Developer Platform.

The [Final Project](1.4%20Final%20Project.md) grows throughout the pathway; track increments on the [Final Project Checklist](1.5%20Final%20Project%20Checklist.md). The final learning activities involve **technical implementation**, not administration-only tasks.

**Learning content maturity:** The DevOps course-detail documents and the full eight-chapter textbook ([D01](../4%20Study%20Materials/4.1%20DevOps/D01%20Repository%20Fundamentals.md)–[D08](../4%20Study%20Materials/4.1%20DevOps/D08%20Datadog.md)) are available for study. The [DevOps syllabus](../3%20Course%20Detail/3.1%20DevOps/D00%20DevOps%20Syllabus.md) summarises the recommended sequence; see also the [DevOps Exit Assessment](../4%20Study%20Materials/4.1%20DevOps/DevOps%20Exit%20Assessment.md). Cloud Engineering has a [discipline overview](../3%20Course%20Detail/3.2%20Cloud%20Engineering/CLOUD%20Overview.md), signed course-detail for [C01](../3%20Course%20Detail/3.2%20Cloud%20Engineering/C01%20Linux%20Networking%20and%20Cloud%20Fundamentals.md)–[C03](../3%20Course%20Detail/3.2%20Cloud%20Engineering/C03%20AWS%20Infrastructure%20and%20Architecture.md), and textbook chapters [C01](../4%20Study%20Materials/4.2%20Cloud%20Engineering/C01%20Linux%20Networking%20and%20Cloud%20Fundamentals.md)–[C02](../4%20Study%20Materials/4.2%20Cloud%20Engineering/C02%20AWS%20Cloud%20Foundations.md); the C03 textbook is not yet authored. Published hour ranges are **planning assumptions** until clean-room completions record actual hours. AI and Platform course-detail modules remain **working outlines** without textbooks.

**Prerequisite bridges (DevOps before Cloud / Kubernetes depth):** D05 teaches enough Terraform/AWS to complete its labs; skim [C01](../3%20Course%20Detail/3.2%20Cloud%20Engineering/C01%20Linux%20Networking%20and%20Cloud%20Fundamentals.md)–[C02](../3%20Course%20Detail/3.2%20Cloud%20Engineering/C02%20AWS%20Cloud%20Foundations.md) outlines for IAM, VPC and billing vocabulary if those are new — do not postpone D05 for full Cloud coursework. D06 uses kind/Kubernetes enough for GitOps; skim Deployments, Services and namespaces (later P01–P02 deepen this) if those objects are unfamiliar. Deeper Cloud and Platform Engineering curricula still own the full treatment.
