## How the learning builds

- **Weeks 1–15:** Build, package, deploy and observe a sample application.
- **Weeks 16–31:** Run it on secure, resilient cloud infrastructure.
- **Weeks 32–43:** Add a carefully scoped AI capability and learn how to evaluate and operate it.
- **Weeks 44–64:** Turn the accumulated capabilities into a reusable Internal Developer Platform.

The project grows throughout the pathway; the final weeks are **technical learning and implementation**, not administration-only weeks.

**Study material maturity:** The [original DevOps syllabus](../3%20Study%20Materials/3.1%20DevOps/DevOps_15_Week_Syllabus.md) contains detailed weekly objectives and labs. The Cloud, AI and Platform module files are **working outlines** and still need equivalent detail. Their current titles and week allocations establish the intended sequence, not a completed syllabus.
