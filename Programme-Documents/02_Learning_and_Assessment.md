# Learning & Assessment

## Weekly method

Spend approximately **8–10 hours per instructional week** studying concepts, completing labs, troubleshooting and recording what worked.

## Evidence of competence

| Area | Example evidence |
| --- | --- |
| DevOps | Containers, CI/CD runs, Terraform and GitOps |
| Cloud | Secure infrastructure, networking and architecture |
| AI | Working integrations, tests, evaluation and safeguards |
| Platform | Kubernetes deployments and developer self-service |
| Overall | Documentation, design decisions and troubleshooting notes |

Complete a module by demonstrating its learning objectives and practical work. Check **correctness, security, repeatability, reliability and understanding**.

The [original DevOps syllabus](../DevOps_15_Week_Syllabus.md) retains its exact weightings (**7%, 20%, 13%, 27%, 20%, 13%**). Later discipline modules still need specific lab criteria.
