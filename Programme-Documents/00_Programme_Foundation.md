### 02. Background and Purpose

This programme builds on existing software development skills to meet broader modern engineering demands. Rather than acting as a collection of disconnected courses, it provides a sequential pathway where each discipline builds directly on the previous one.

**Core Goal:** To equip engineers to design, build, deploy, operate, and continuously improve resilient software systems—as well as the internal tools developers use to deliver them.

---

### 03. Programme Philosophy

The pathway begins with **DevOps** because its practices deliver immediate day-to-day value to a software developer. Subsequent disciplines expand outward from that operational foundation:

| Discipline | Core Contribution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **DevOps** | Source control, containerisation, CI/CD, automation, and observability |
| **Cloud** | Infrastructure, networking, identity, security, and resilient architecture |
| **AI** | Intelligent applications, autonomous agents, and AI operations (AIOps) |
| **Platform** | Self-service tooling, standardised delivery, and developer experience |

### 05. Objectives and Learning Outcomes

#### Programme Objectives
* **Build practical capability** across DevOps, Cloud, AI, and Platform Engineering.
* **Embed operational rigour** through security, reliability, maintainability, and cost controls.
* **Validate knowledge** by pursuing relevant, industry-recognised certifications.
* **Demonstrate mastery** via a portfolio of working systems and documented architectural decisions.

#### Learning Outcomes
Upon completion, participants will be able to:
* **Automate** testing, container builds, deployment pipelines, and infrastructure provisioning.
* **Operate** cloud services while enforcing sound networking, identity, and security principles.
* **Implement** end-to-end observability (monitoring, logging, tracing) and operational recovery.
* **Integrate** and evaluate applied AI capabilities within software and operational workflows.
* **Deploy** and troubleshoot production-grade Kubernetes workloads.
* **Build** reusable developer self-service capabilities and justify architectural trade-offs.

Detailed, assessable criteria are maintained in [Programme Learning Outcomes](04_Programme_Learning_Outcomes.md) and individual module specifications.

### 06. Teaching and Learning Methodology

* **Weekly Allocation:** Dedicate approximately **8–10 hours per instructional week** across conceptual study, guided tutorials, hands-on labs, troubleshooting, and technical documentation.
* **Environment Hygiene:** Conduct all practical work in non-confidential, sandboxed environments in strict compliance with employer and client security policies.

---

### 07. Practical Capstone Project

An organisation-neutral project evolves iteratively across all four stages of the programme:

1. **DevOps:** Containerised application, automated CI/CD pipelines, Terraform provisioning, GitOps workflows, and baseline monitoring.
2. **Cloud:** Hardened cloud infrastructure, segmented networking, managed cloud services, and high-availability resilience.
3. **AI:** Applied intelligent assistance, workflow automation, or AI-driven operational analysis.
4. **Platform:** Integrated self-service workflows, reusable service templates, golden paths, and end-to-end reliability controls.

The project must remain completely independent of confidential employer or client systems. Full scope and acceptance criteria are defined in the [Capstone Specification](08_Capstone_Project.md).

### 09. Assessment Philosophy

* **Portfolio of Evidence:** Competency is demonstrated through working code, infrastructure-as-code (IaC) definitions, CI/CD pipelines, observability dashboards, completed labs, Architecture Decision Records (ADRs), and live demonstrations.
* **Evaluation Criteria:** Work is assessed on correctness, security, repeatability, operational reliability, and clarity of technical reasoning.
* **Governance:** Detailed grading rubrics reside in the [Assessment Framework](07_Assessment_Framework.md). Formal evaluations and sign-offs occur outside the 64 instructional weeks.
