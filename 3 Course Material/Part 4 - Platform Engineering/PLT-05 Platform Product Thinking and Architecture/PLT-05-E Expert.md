## MODULE PLT-05-E — PLATFORM PRODUCT THINKING AND ARCHITECTURE — EXPERT

### 1. Topic and Scope

**In scope for this level:** Evaluating platform strategy, adoption and architectural trade-offs. You treat the platform as a product organisation, define the thinnest viable platform, choose team topology and service-versus-capability boundaries, compare build, adopt and buy, anticipate organisational failure modes and defend an architecture to a reviewer who has read the records.

**Out of scope:** Writing personas and briefs (Beginner), drawing boundaries (Intermediate), outcome matrices (Professional), vendor selection procurement detail and company-specific financial modelling.

**Self-assessment fit:** Choose this level in [2.2 Topic Self Assessment](../../../2%20PDP%20Development/2.2%20Topic%20Self%20Assessment.md#plt-05--platform-product-thinking-and-architecture) when this matches your current ability: *Evaluate platform strategy, adoption and architectural trade-offs.* Typical starting point: You can design a platform and now need to judge when to expand, buy, deprecate or leave something out.

### 2. Learning Objectives

1. Define the thinnest viable platform for a team of ten engineers and justify each included capability by the user problem it removes.
2. Compare building, adopting open source and buying for one capability using total cost, cognitive load, lock-in, exit cost and adoption risk, not licence price alone.
3. Choose between providing a capability as a service, a library, or documentation, and explain how the choice changes support load and autonomy.
4. Anticipate three organisational failure modes (ivory-tower platform, mandated adoption, funding collapse) and state the early warning signal and response for each.
5. Prepare the evidence needed for **PRJ-088**: a platform-evolution critique covering build-versus-buy, cognitive load, adoption, funding and boundaries with concrete trade-offs.

### 4. Knowledge Assessment

**Task:** Defend a decision to keep your platform deliberately small against a request to add a developer portal, an internal database service and a policy dashboard. State the evidence that would change your answer and three explicit non-goals.

**Pass criteria:** Trade-offs include cost, adoption and exit; non-goals are genuine; the response uses user evidence rather than preference; at least one capability is deferred with a trigger for revisiting.

Record the result in your Learning Progress Record. Passing the knowledge assessment does **not** by itself complete the module — the project assignment below is also required for a project-linked pathway.
