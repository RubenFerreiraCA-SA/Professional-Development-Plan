## MODULE PLT-06-E — GOLDEN PATHS, TEMPLATES AND DEVELOPER EXPERIENCE — EXPERT

### 1. Topic and Scope

**In scope for this level:** Evaluating platform usability, adoption and support metrics. You run an independent trial correctly (qualified participants, task cards, silent observer rules, intervention levels I2 and I3, PASS, FAIL and INVALID outcomes), report honest statistics for very small samples, convert stuck points into a ranked backlog, scale template governance without copy-and-diverge, and use DORA and developer-experience signals without turning them into targets.

**Out of scope:** Writing the first-use guide and templates (Beginner to Intermediate), designing the measurement protocol and status commands (Professional), and portal vendor procurement and company-specific survey tooling.

**Self-assessment fit:** Choose this level in [2.2 Topic Self Assessment](../../../2%20PDP%20Development/2.2%20Topic%20Self%20Assessment.md#plt-06--golden-paths-templates-and-developer-experience) when this matches your current ability: *Evaluate platform usability, adoption and support metrics.* Typical starting point: You have run a cold read and now need to judge whether a platform is genuinely usable and how to prove it without fooling yourself.

### 2. Learning Objectives

1. Set up an independent trial that is valid: qualify the participant, prepare the clean accounts and a planted safe failure, write task cards with no hints, and state the observer rules, including what to do when asked a question.
2. Classify a trial outcome as PASS, FAIL (intervention), FAIL (blocked) or INVALID using the I2 and I3 intervention rules, and explain why a returning participant or a leaked author hint invalidates a result.
3. Report a one- or two-participant trial honestly, using counts and verbatim quotes rather than percentages, and explain why an LLM docs probe is not a trial.
4. Turn stuck points into a ranked backlog with source, owner and verification step, then choose which finding to fix first and how to re-verify it with a new participant.
5. Prepare the evidence needed for **PRJ-092**: a self-service effectiveness critique covering feedback, discoverability, support burden and how cognitive load would be measured, with DORA-style signals treated as signals rather than targets.

### 4. Knowledge Assessment

**Task:** A participant could not find the access-request page and you answered the question. Explain what the result is now, what you must do, which platform changes you would consider and in what order, and how you would decide whether to retire part of the golden path.

**Pass criteria:** The result is correctly classed as FAIL (intervention) and the evidence is not rescued; a new participant is planned; fixes are ranked with owners and verification; metric definitions stay fixed after the trial; the reasoning separates platform problems from participant problems.

Record the result in your Learning Progress Record. Passing the knowledge assessment does **not** by itself complete the module — the project assignment below is also required for a project-linked pathway.
