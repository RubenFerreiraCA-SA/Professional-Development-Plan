# T-PLT-05 — Platform Product Thinking and Architecture

*Part 4 — Platform Engineering*

A platform is a product whose customers are other engineers. Understanding who they are, what jobs they hire it for, what it promises and what it costs explains why some platforms are adopted and others are quietly bypassed.

## Levels


- [Beginner Foundations](T-PLT-05-B%20Beginner.md#t-plt-05-b) — platform versus tool pile, internal users and jobs, cognitive load, golden paths, team boundaries, product brief, user-outcome metrics.
- [Intermediate Practice](T-PLT-05-I%20Intermediate.md#t-plt-05-i) — user research, service boundaries and the platform contract, catalogue, discovery, metrics, onboarding, prioritisation, architecture as text and ADRs.
- [Professional Engineering](T-PLT-05-P%20Professional.md#t-plt-05-p) — product strategy, ADRs at scale, team interfaces, SLOs, roadmaps, funding and cost, trust claims, outcome measures.
- [Expert Depth](T-PLT-05-E%20Expert.md#t-plt-05-e) — operating models, economics, multi-tenancy, governance versus autonomy, evolution, migration, incentive failures, defence and cases.

All examples use neutral fictional services and teams; the chapter has no dependency on any other course material.
