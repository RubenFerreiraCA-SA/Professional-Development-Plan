# Progress and Evidence Log

This log answers: **what have I learned and completed?** It is not the calendar plan and not the platform acceptance spec.

| View | Artefact | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan | [Capacity Planner](PDP%20Capacity%20Planner.xlsx) | When do I expect to finish? |
| **Learning (this file)** | Progress and Evidence Log | What have I learned and completed? |
| Platform | [Final Project Checklist](../1%20Foundational%20Documents/1.5%20Final%20Project%20Checklist.md) | What can the platform demonstrably do? |

Use working evidence as the measure of learning progress, not hours elapsed. Mark a module **Complete** here when you can demonstrate its Course Detail outcomes. Tick the Final Project Checklist only when that row’s **acceptance criterion** passes (often a stricter, second-person or cold-run bar).

Duplicate rows as needed, or use sheet **4. Progress Log** in [`PDP Capacity Planner.xlsx`](PDP%20Capacity%20Planner.xlsx).
