## Four competency levels

| Level | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Beginner** | Recognise terms, explain basics, guided first task |
| **Intermediate** | Routine independent work and common troubleshooting |
| **Professional** | Reliable design/operation with defensible decisions |
| **Expert** | Complex trade-offs, failure modes, mentoring-quality analysis |

Levels are competency descriptors, not job titles or years of experience.

## Independence rules

- The **textbook** does not depend on the course or project.
- The **project** does not depend on the course or textbook.
- The **course** links to exact textbook sections and project steps.
- The **PDP Development** worksheets select modules and track progress; they do not rewrite the texts.

## Optional tracks

You may follow the full pathway or an adapted pathway that marks an entire optional track (for example Datadog `PRJ-025`–`PRJ-028`, or AI `PRJ-049`–`PRJ-068`) **Out of scope**.

Within every **active** route, project steps remain mandatory and ordered. You may never mark an individual step Out of scope inside a track you are taking.

**Prerequisite and gate rule:** a required earlier step is satisfied when it is recorded **Completed**, or **Out of scope** because the whole optional track that contains it was excluded. Later modules (for example Platform Engineering) that list prerequisites spanning an excluded optional track treat those Out-of-scope steps as satisfied for gate purposes — they do not unlock skipping steps in tracks you kept active.

## Study hours

Hour estimates are planning aids only. Recalculate after you choose modules and project catch-up steps. Do not treat legacy 18-unit hour totals as a forecast for this catalogue.
