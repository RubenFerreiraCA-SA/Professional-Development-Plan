# 1.1 Programme Vision

The Professional Development Plan (PDP) is a **self-directed educational programme** for software engineers who want structured growth across DevOps, Cloud, AI and Platform Engineering.

## What this programme is

You self-assess every topic as Beginner, Intermediate, Professional or Expert; follow only the course modules that match those ratings; study from a fully independent master textbook; and complete sequential practical tasks in a separately understandable project — including prerequisite project tasks for any theory modules you skipped.

## What this programme is not

- Not a university degree or external certification.
- Not a commercial product delivery contract.
- Not a requirement to build a production-grade Internal Developer Platform.
- Not an engineering monorepo. This repository holds **learning texts and planning worksheets only**.

## Learning outcome

A capable engineer who can explain foundations, apply reliable practice, make defensible decisions and continue learning independently — with evidence of practical work proportionate to an educational project, not enterprise certification.

## Harbour

Harbour is the name of the **example student project** described in the Project Document. You implement it in your own workspace if you follow the practical route. It does not dictate the structure of the master textbook.
