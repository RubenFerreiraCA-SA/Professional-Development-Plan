# 1.4 Assessment And Progression Rules

## Topic self-assessment

- Every one of the 24 topics receives exactly one level: Beginner, Intermediate, Professional or Expert.
- Record a short evidence note (what you can explain or demonstrate).
- Self-assessment personalises reading; it is not proof that project work was done.

## Entry routing

| Self-rating | Start module |
| --- | --- |
| Beginner | `TOPIC-B` |
| Intermediate | `TOPIC-I` (do not force Beginner theory) |
| Professional | `TOPIC-P` (do not force B/I theory) |
| Expert | `TOPIC-E` (still complete the Expert module) |

Apply independently per topic.

## Course module completion

A module on a **project-linked pathway** is complete only when:

1. Entry gate project prerequisites are recorded complete.
2. Knowledge assessment pass criteria are met.
3. The module’s assigned project step is recorded complete.

Knowledge assessment and project completion are **separate records**.

## Project rules

- Full route: `PRJ-001`–`PRJ-096` in order.
- Adapted route: an entire optional track may be Out of scope once in the PDP; no casual step skipping inside an active route.
- Academic-only adapted pathway (no project) must be marked explicitly; do not claim project-linked module completion.

## Progress vocabulary

**Course:** Not assigned (prior knowledge) · Not started · In progress · Assessment passed · Completed  

**Project:** Not started · In progress · Completed · Out of scope (whole unselected track only)
